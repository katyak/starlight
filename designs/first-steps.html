Our Story

Face to face, step by step

Nestled in the heart of Silicon Valley, Starlite Ballroom is where world-class coaching meets a warm, welcoming community. Whether you're stepping onto the floor for the very first time or training for competition — you'll find a home here.

Our award-winning instructors bring passion, precision, and playfulness to every lesson. Come as you are. Leave dancing.
