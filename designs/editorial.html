Our Story

Where coaching meets community

Nestled in the heart of Silicon Valley, Starlite Ballroom is where world-class instruction meets a warm, welcoming community. Whether you're stepping onto the floor for the very first time or training for your next competition — you'll find a home here.

Dancing isn't just a skill — it's a way of expressing who you are. Come as you are. Leave transformed.
