Starlite Ballroom ×8 Redesign Concepts

Eight distinct visual directions for the Starlite Ballroom website — all built from the studio's real, up-to-date info (June 2026 calendar, current event posters, programs, 20 dance styles, teachers, hours, contact). Concepts 6–8 follow styles drawn from research into the best dance-studio websites. Click any concept to explore the full page, then share your favorite.

Golden Age

Black-and-gold Art Deco glamour. Cinzel serifs, gilded frames, and a Gatsby-era ballroom mood. Closest in spirit to the current dark draft, but more luxurious and editorial.
