Starlite Ballroom ×10 Redesign Concepts

The original redesign plus ten distinct visual directions for the Starlite Ballroom website — all built from the studio's real, up-to-date info (June 2026 calendar, current event posters, real studio videos, programs, 20 dance styles, teachers, hours, contact). The first two concepts (and the last) are built around the studio's own photography; several others follow styles drawn from research into standout dance-studio websites.
