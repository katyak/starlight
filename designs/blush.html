Our Story

A warmer way to learn to dance

· · ·

Nestled in the heart of Silicon Valley, Starlite Ballroom is where world-class coaching meets a genuinely warm community. Whether you're stepping onto the floor for the very first time, preparing for your wedding day, or training for competition — you'll find a home here.

Our award-winning instructors bring passion, precision, and playfulness to every lesson. Dancing isn't just a skill — it's a way of expressing who you are. Come as you are. Leave transformed.
