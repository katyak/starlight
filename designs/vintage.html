Our Story

An Old-Fashioned Welcome

★ ★ ★

Nestled in the heart of Silicon Valley, Starlite Ballroom is where world-class coaching meets a warm, welcoming community — the kind of place where the music is always playing and there's always room on the floor.

Whether you're stepping out for the very first time or training for your next competition, our award-winning instructors bring passion, precision, and playfulness to every lesson. Come as you are. Leave dancing.
