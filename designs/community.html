Our Story

A studio that feels like home

Nestled in the heart of Silicon Valley, Starlite Ballroom is where world-class coaching meets a warm, welcoming community. Whether it's your very first step or your next competition, you'll find a home here.

Our instructors bring passion, precision, and playfulness to every lesson — because dancing should always feel like a celebration. Come as you are. Leave dancing.
