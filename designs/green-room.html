Our Studio

A joyful, non-judgmental place to move

Nestled in the heart of Silicon Valley, Starlite Ballroom is where world-class coaching meets a warm, welcoming community. Whether it's your very first step or your next competition, you'll find a home — and a floor — here.

Twenty dances. Ten teachers. Social parties every weekend. And a community that will cheer your first cha cha as loudly as your hundredth.
