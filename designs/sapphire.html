Our Story

Two dancers, one floor

Nestled in the heart of Silicon Valley, Starlite Ballroom is where world-class coaching meets a warm, welcoming community. Whether you're stepping onto the floor for the very first time or training for your next title — you'll find a home here.

Our award-winning instructors bring the passion, precision, and playfulness that makes every lesson feel like a celebration. Come as you are. Leave transformed.
