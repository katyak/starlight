Our Story

Where the evening turns golden

Nestled in the heart of Silicon Valley, Starlite Ballroom is where world-class coaching meets a warm, welcoming community. Whether you're stepping onto the floor for the very first time or chasing your next title — you'll find a home here.

Our award-winning instructors bring the passion, precision, and playfulness that makes every lesson feel like a celebration. Come as you are. Leave transformed.

"Dance is the hidden language of the soul." — Martha Graham

This Is Starlite

One floor. One family.

Real students, real Friday nights — from first-timers to competitors, everyone shares the same floor and the same joy. No partner needed. No judgment, ever.
