Our Story

An Evening at Starlite

✦

Nestled in the heart of Silicon Valley, Starlite Ballroom is where world-class coaching meets a warm, welcoming community. Whether you're stepping onto the floor for the very first time or training for your next competition — you'll find a home here.

Our award-winning instructors bring the passion, precision, and playfulness that makes every lesson feel like a celebration. We believe dancing isn't just a skill — it's a way of expressing who you are. Come as you are. Leave transformed.

"Dance is the hidden language of the soul." — Martha Graham

Our Programs

✦
I

Private Lessons

One-on-one instruction tailored to your goals, schedule, and style. Personal attention, real results — and your first lesson is free.

II

Social Dancing

Drop-in classes, practice parties, and social dance nights in a warm, judgment-free space. Friday and Saturday evenings shine brightest.

III

Wedding Dance

Make your first dance unforgettable. Beautiful choreography crafted to your song and your love story — completely stress-free.

IV

Competitive

Train with coaches who have competed at the highest international levels. Technique, artistry, and the edge to shine on stage.

V

Kids & Teens

Confidence, coordination, and a love of movement. Age-appropriate weekend classes that build a foundation for life.
