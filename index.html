Nestled in the heart of Silicon Valley, Starlite Ballroom is where world-class coaching meets a warm, welcoming community. Whether you're stepping onto the floor for the very first time or training for your next competition — you'll find a home here.

Our Promise

Why Starlite?

World-Class Coaches

Our instructors have competed and trained at the highest levels of international ballroom, bringing elite expertise and genuine passion to every session.

Welcoming Atmosphere

From nervous first-timers to seasoned competitors, everyone belongs here. Our studio is a judgment-free space built on encouragement, laughter, and joy.

All Levels Welcome

No experience? No problem. Our structured programs grow with you — from your very first step all the way to competition-ready routines and performance.

Silicon Valley's Studio

Conveniently located in West San Jose, minutes from Cupertino and Sunnyvale — a world-class dance experience at the heart of the Bay Area tech community.
